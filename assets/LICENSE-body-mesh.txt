assets/body-mesh.js — license and provenance

The athletes' base meshes are derived from the MakeHuman 1.1 base mesh ("hm08", makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj)
and MakeHuman macro targets (makehuman/data/targets/macrodetails/...), fetched from
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman (master).

The MakeHuman project released these assets under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
"These assets have been released under CC0 1.0 Universal ... to the fullest extent possible, it is the
intention of the MakeHuman project that anyone can do whatever they want with it." (MakeHuman LICENSE.md,
section C; full text in LICENSE.ASSETS.md of that repository; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Original copyright holders at the time of the CC0 release: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier
(original copyright Manuel Bastioni, 2014).

Changes made here (tools/build-body-mesh.js): targets applied for a young adult male and female, helper
geometry removed, face features and ears removed and the surface faired (faceless), nipples and groin
smoothed, skin weights computed for this site's skeleton, and the mesh moved onto that skeleton's rest pose.
No MakeHuman code is included or run by the site. Muscle relief, the muscle map and fiber directions are
added procedurally at runtime (js/figure3d.js), not part of the MakeHuman asset.
